Bankroll Alert Margins

Set percentage thresholds for bankroll alerts. When your available bankroll drops below these margins of your total deposits, you'll get notified.

Example: 5% means if you deposited TZS 1,000,000 and your balance drops to TZS 950,000, you get a CRITICAL alert.
Example: 10% means if you deposited TZS 1,000,000 and your balance drops to TZS 900,000, you get a WARNING alert.
How it works: The system monitors your total balance vs total deposits across all bookmakers. If balance < deposits × (1 - margin%), you get an alert. RED = critical, YELLOW = warning.
